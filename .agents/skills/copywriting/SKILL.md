## Copywriting

Expert conversion copywriting focused on clear, compelling marketing copy that drives action.

### Before Writing

Check for existing product marketing context in `.agents/product-marketing-context.md` or `.claude/product-marketing-context.md` before asking questions.

Required context gathering:

1. **Page Purpose** — Type of page and primary visitor action
2. **Audience** — Ideal customer, problem they solve, objections, their language
3. **Product/Offer** — What's being sold, differentiation, transformation, proof
4. **Context** — Traffic source and visitor prior knowledge

### Core Principles

- Clarity over cleverness
- Benefits over features
- Specificity over vagueness (e.g., "Cut reporting from 4 hours to 15 minutes" vs. "save time")
- Customer language over company jargon
- One idea per section

### Writing Style Rules

1. Simple over complex
2. Specific over vague (avoid "streamline," "optimize")
3. Active over passive voice
4. Confident (remove qualifiers like "almost," "very")
5. Show outcomes rather than tell with adverbs
6. Honest over sensational

### Best Practices

- **Be Direct:** Get to point immediately
- **Use Rhetorical Questions:** Engage readers about their situation
- **Use Analogies:** Make abstract concepts concrete
- **Humor (When Appropriate):** Make copy memorable if brand-appropriate

### Page Structure Framework

**Above the Fold:**
- Headline communicating core value
- Subheadline expanding with specificity
- Primary CTA with action + benefit

**Core Sections:**
- Social Proof (credibility building)
- Problem/Pain (show understanding)
- Solution/Benefits (3-5 key outcomes)
- How It Works (3-4 steps)
- Objection Handling (FAQs, comparisons, guarantees)
- Final CTA (value recap, risk reversal)

### CTA Guidelines

Avoid: Submit, Sign Up, Learn More, Click Here

Use: Start Free Trial, Get [Specific Thing], See [Product] in Action

Formula: [Action Verb] + [What They Get] + [Qualifier if needed]

### Page-Specific Guidance

- **Homepage:** Multiple audience paths without being generic
- **Landing Page:** Single message, single CTA, match ad headlines
- **Pricing Page:** Help choose right plan, address anxiety
- **Feature Page:** Feature → benefit → outcome connection
- **About Page:** Story of why you exist, mission to customer benefit

### Voice and Tone

Establish formality level (casual, professional-friendly, formal) and brand personality before writing. Maintain consistency while adjusting intensity by section.

### Output Format

Provide:
- Page copy organized by section
- Annotations explaining choices
- 2-3 alternatives for headlines/CTAs with rationale
- Meta content if relevant
